If you’re not willing to invest in a product continuously, or you don’t have a solid plan to promote it, it’s better not to start at all. Don’t rely on “organic growth,” even if it’s a product you personally need. If there’s real demand, a better competitor will almost certainly appear sooner or later. When that happens, you’ll be forced to choose between switching to the competitor or doubling down on improving your own product. And if your product has no users and isn’t as good as the competing option, chances are you’ll end up choosing the competitor yourself.
